
## Production foundation expansion
The release adds an additive MySQL data model and protected tRPC procedures for family circles, member roles, invitations, appointments, tasks, voice-note drafts, human review, visit notes, consent, notification preferences, family documents, emergency contacts, and audit history. The dashboard now exposes a production workspace for care timeline, reminder channels and quiet hours, privacy/consent, care-load balance, availability context, offline queue state, older-adult mode, emergency guidance, practical family records, and sign-in-to-sync. External delivery providers (SMS, email, push, calendar accounts, and storage) remain explicit integration seams rather than being claimed as connected; the calendar procedure emits an ICS export and all AI-derived information remains a human-reviewed draft.
